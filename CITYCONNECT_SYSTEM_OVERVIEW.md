# CityConnect: The Ultimate Smart City Super App

CityConnect is a unified, omnichannel "Super App" designed to digitize and connect an entire municipal economy. Rather than residents juggling 15 different apps for rides, groceries, schools, and events, CityConnect brings them all into a single, cohesive platform powered by a unified payment and identity layer.

The platform is divided into two massive halves: the **Resident Consumer App** and the **Provider Admin Ecosystem**.

---

## 1. The Core Infrastructure

*   **CityWallet**: The financial heartbeat of the platform. A centralized digital wallet where residents store funds, tap-to-pay at physical locations, and seamlessly check out across all digital verticals.
*   **CityID**: A unified identity layer ensuring one resident has one secure account for everything from registering their child for school to booking a doctor's appointment.

---

## 2. The Resident Consumer App (The Front-End)
This is the interface the everyday citizen uses. We have built 8 distinct, industry-leading verticals:

1.  **CityMall (Grocery & Retail)**: An *Instacart-style* digital marketplace where residents can buy from any local store, with unified CityRide delivery.
2.  **Events & Ticketing**: An *Eventbrite-style* discovery feed for local concerts, workshops, and meetups, with QR tickets saved directly to CityWallet.
3.  **Education & Schools**: A *GreatSchools-style* directory allowing parents to filter local schools, view academic scorecards, and read reviews.
4.  **Accommodations**: A *Booking.com-style* interface for tourists and residents to book local boutique hotels and B&Bs.
5.  **Real Estate & Rentals**: A *Zillow-style* map view for finding local apartments and properties.
6.  **Healthcare**: A *Zocdoc-style* booking interface to find specialists, check symptoms, and book available clinic slots.
7.  **Local Services**: A *TaskRabbit-style* directory for hiring verified local plumbers, electricians, and cleaners.
8.  **CityRide (Mobility)**: An *Uber-style* interface for hailing rides or tracking inbound CityMall deliveries.

---

## 3. The Provider Admin Ecosystem (The Back-End)
This is where the magic happens. We are building bespoke, industry-standard management software for the businesses powering the city, given to them for free to run their operations on CityConnect.

### 🏫 School Management System (Classe365 Model)
*   **Super Admin / Registrar / Counselor**: Dashboards for managing admissions, transcripts, and student well-being.
*   **Teacher Portal**: Features a full Learning Management System (LMS) for quizzes, grading, and discussions.
*   **Student Portal**: A unified dashboard for students to view grades, take quizzes, and buy physical items.
*   **Finance Portal**: Features a "School Store" E-Commerce setup for selling spirit wear and textbooks via CityWallet.

### 🏨 Hotel Management System (Little Hotelier Model)
*   **Front Desk Calendar (PMS)**: A visual, drag-and-drop timeline for managing check-ins and CityWallet payments.
*   **General Manager Dashboard**: A dynamic rate engine for setting weekend/holiday surge pricing and tracking RevPAR.
*   **Housekeeping App**: A mobile-first UI for cleaners to toggle room statuses (Dirty -> Clean), syncing instantly to the front desk.

### 🛒 Grocery & Retail POS (Instacart + Square Model)
*   **Omnichannel Store Manager**: A dashboard to push physical inventory to the digital CityMall marketplace and track revenue splits.
*   **In-Store POS**: A tablet-optimized Point of Sale register for walk-in customers to tap-to-pay with CityWallet.
*   **Order Picker App**: A mobile app for floor staff to walk the aisles, pick CityMall digital orders, and handle out-of-stock replacements.
*   **Dispatch Hub**: A staging UI to hand off packed bags to CityRide drivers.

### 🎟️ Events Ticketing (Eventbrite + DICE Model)
*   **Event Organizer Dashboard**: A command center for promoters to create events, set up VIP/GA ticket tiers, and track revenue conversions.
*   **Door Scanner App**: A high-speed, dark-mode mobile app for bouncers to scan QR tickets, manually look up guest lists, and track live venue capacity.
